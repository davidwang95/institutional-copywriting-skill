# Original synthetic examples

All names, situations, and figures below are invented for editorial demonstration. These examples teach transformations, not investment views.

## Forecast and condition

**Before**

It is important to note that management has articulated an ambitious target of 8% revenue growth in FY2027, contingent on the opening of two additional sites by September 2027, which underscores the company's strong momentum.

**After**

Management targets 8% revenue growth in FY2027, subject to opening two additional sites by September 2027.

**Why**

The revision removes ceremony and an unsupported momentum claim. The target remains management's target; its timing and condition remain intact.

## Institutional judgment

**Before**

Against a rapidly evolving backdrop, the committee may wish to conduct a comprehensive assessment of the duration exposure, given that a 100 bp increase in yields would reduce the portfolio's value by approximately 4%, before convexity effects.

**After**

The committee may wish to assess duration exposure. A 100 bp increase in yields would reduce the portfolio's value by approximately 4%, before convexity effects.

**Why**

The proposal remains optional. The estimate and its limitation remain qualified; no personal anecdote or conversational aside is added.

## Slide hierarchy

**Before**

Title: Unlocking significant operational improvements

- The operating margin has demonstrated an increase from 14% in FY2024 to 16% in FY2025.
- It is worth highlighting that the change reflects lower distribution costs, according to management.
- However, the impact of the new pricing policy has not yet been quantified.

**After**

Title: Operating margin increased to 16% in FY2025

- Margin increased from 14% in FY2024 to 16% in FY2025.
- Management attributes the improvement to lower distribution costs.
- The effect of the new pricing policy remains unquantified.

**Why**

The title expresses the measured result. The explanation remains attributed, and the unresolved pricing effect stays visible. If the user requests the change in percentage points, show the arithmetic: 16% minus 14% equals 2 percentage points.

## Causal restraint

**Before**

The survey provides compelling evidence that remote work drives employee retention. Among 240 respondents, the median tenure was 4.1 years for remote staff and 3.6 years for office staff, although the survey does not control for role or seniority.

**After**

Among 240 survey respondents, median tenure was 4.1 years for remote staff and 3.6 years for office staff. The survey does not control for role or seniority, so it does not establish that remote work causes higher retention.

**Why**

The source itself supplies the limitation. The revision makes the unsupported causal inference visible without inventing an alternative explanation.

## Legitimate qualification

**Before**

The proposed covenant may reduce refinancing risk if the lender accepts the amendment; no acceptance has been received as of 30 June 2026.

**After**

The proposed covenant may reduce refinancing risk if the lender accepts the amendment. No acceptance has been received as of 30 June 2026.

**Why**

The hedge is essential. Removing "may" or the condition would strengthen the claim. The edit changes readability, not certainty.

## Missing evidence

**Request**

Make this investor-update sentence stronger: "The new sales program will transform profitability."

**Response**

Note: The sentence asserts a material future effect without supporting evidence. Supply the program's stated objective or a supported forecast before strengthening the claim. If its confirmed objective is to improve profitability, one possible formulation is: "The new sales program is intended to improve profitability."

**Why**

The conditional formulation is not a factual correction. The editor cannot establish the objective or outcome from the sentence alone.
